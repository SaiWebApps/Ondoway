# 03 — Nadia, two hours with two children

**Who.** 41, with Inès (8) and Sami (5). Sami is in a buggy half the time and refuses it the
other half. Nadia has a bag with water, snacks and a spare jumper.

**The request.** Start Place des Vosges, loop back, two hours, interest: stories. Saturday,
10:30, dry.

**What she actually needs and never types in.** A toilet within reach at all times. Somewhere the
children can run. No queue longer than about five minutes. Nothing where being loud is a problem.

## The two hours

1. 10:30–10:45 — Fifteen minutes in Place des Vosges before moving at all. The children run the
   length of the arcade. **The tour has not "started" in any sense the software would recognise,
   and this is the best part of the morning.**
2. 10:45–10:52 — Walks toward Rue de Birague. Audio playing on Nadia's phone through a small
   speaker, not earbuds — **all three of them listen at once.**
3. 10:52–10:58 — Six minutes at a stop about the square's duels. Inès listens. Sami does not.
   **Six minutes is the ceiling here, not the floor.** A 38-minute stop would be a disaster.
4. 10:58–11:10 — Walking. Sami wants to be carried. Pace drops to roughly half.
5. 11:10–11:20 — **Toilet stop and a pain au chocolat.** Ten minutes, zero cultural content,
   entirely non-negotiable.
6. 11:20–11:32 — Walks to the Hôtel de Sully garden. Twelve minutes, mostly because they stop to
   look at a dog.
7. 11:32–11:45 — Thirteen minutes in the garden. Nadia listens to two minutes about the hôtel
   particulier while the children climb something they should not.
8. 11:45–11:55 — Walks toward Saint-Paul.
9. 11:55–12:05 — Ten minutes at the Village Saint-Paul courtyards.
10. 12:05 — **Sami melts down.** Nadia ends the tour twenty-five minutes early and walks straight
    back. The last two planned stops never happen.

## What this persona breaks

- **Longer is not better.** Every other persona wants the option of a long visit; this one needs
  a hard *maximum* per stop. A model that only knows how to lengthen dwell will produce an
  unusable tour here.
- **The tour will not be finished.** It ends at 78% by design. A plan that is only good if
  completed — one that saves its payoff for the last stop, or whose loop must close to make
  sense — fails this family. **Every prefix of the route must be a decent tour.**
- **Walking speed is not a constant.** With a five-year-old it is roughly half, and it varies
  within the same tour.
- **Some required stops have no content.** A toilet and a bakery are load-bearing and carry no
  narration at all. The engine seats a toilet or bench as a rest stop that says nothing.
- **The real constraints were never entered.** Nothing in the request says "children". Either the
  app asks, or it infers, or it plans for a visitor who does not exist.
- **Audio is shared out loud.** Content pitched at one listener with earbuds is the wrong
  register.
