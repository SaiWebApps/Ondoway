# 07 — Aiko, a wet Tuesday

**Who.** 26, in Paris four days, had planned a park afternoon. It has been raining since eleven
and is forecast to keep going. She has a small umbrella and canvas shoes that are already wet.

**The request.** Start Palais-Royal, open walk, three hours, interest: art and design. **Tuesday,
14:00, steady rain.**

**Why this persona exists.** Her request is nearly identical to one Camille might make, and the
right answer is completely different — because of two facts the app never asks for: the weather,
and the day of the week.

## The three hours

1. 14:00–14:20 — Twenty minutes in the Palais-Royal galleries. **Arcaded, so she is dry.** The
   Buren columns are in the open courtyard and she looks at them from under the colonnade rather
   than walking among them.
2. 14:20–14:26 — Six minutes' walk, and she is unhappy for all of it. **A ten-minute leg in this
   weather would be a worse experience than a fifteen-minute leg in sunshine.** Walking has a
   different cost today.
3. 14:26–14:55 — Galerie Vivienne and Galerie Colbert. Twenty-nine minutes. **Covered the entire
   time.** In dry weather she would have given these eight minutes and moved on.
4. 14:55–15:02 — Short hop, mostly under the Passage des Panoramas.
5. 15:02–15:35 — Passage des Panoramas and Passage Jouffroy. Thirty-three minutes, dry, with
   plenty to hear.
6. 15:35–15:50 — **Walks to a museum she wanted to see and finds it shut. It is Tuesday.** Fifteen
   minutes wasted and the tour has lost her trust. This is the failure that matters most in this
   whole document, because it has nothing to do with time arithmetic and everything to do with
   whether the plan was ever real.
7. 15:50–16:10 — Recovers with twenty minutes in a bookshop-café. Not a sight; it is where she
   dries off.
8. 16:10–16:30 — Twenty minutes in the covered part of the Bourse de Commerce approach.
9. 16:30–17:00 — Thirty minutes inside one gallery, sitting down for a good part of it.

## What this persona breaks

- **Start, duration and lens are not enough input.** The same three values produce a good tour on
  a dry Wednesday and a miserable one on a wet Tuesday. Weather and weekday change which stops are
  right, how long each is worth, and what walking costs.
- **Opening hours are not optional.** A route to a locked door is not a slightly worse tour, it is
  a broken one. The corpus records them, and the planner keeps a shut place out of the day.
- **The cost of walking is not fixed.** In rain, legs should be shorter and covered routes worth a
  detour. Today walking has one price everywhere and always.
- **Shelter is a property of a route, not only of a stop.** An arcade is a *covered leg*. There is
  no way to express that a particular stretch of walking is pleasant.
- **A place's value depends on conditions.** Galerie Vivienne is a footnote in sunshine and an
  anchor in rain — a swing as large as Camille and Théo's disagreement about the Cour Carrée, and
  driven by something neither of them declared.
- **Recovering from a failure is part of the product.** Step 7 is where a good app would have
  noticed the closure at planning time, and where a decent one would at least replan on the spot.
