# Interview guide — concept interview, NYC

For the founder. The traveller never sees this. Screen-by-screen prototype script:
`script.md`. Session plan and decisions: `brief.md`. Terms: `Docs/traveller/CONTEXT.md`.

**Three rules for the whole hour**
1. Ask about what they *did*, never what they *would* do, until part 3.
2. Never name a problem before they do. If they don't bring it up, that is data.
3. In part 3, never explain a screen. If you need to, write down which screen: it is a
   prototype defect, not a traveller failure.

**Recording:** every answer below is coded **U** (unprompted: they raised it) or
**P** (prompted: they said it after you asked directly). U and P never get pooled.

---

## 0 · Opening — 2 min

> Thanks for doing this. I'm working on something for people who travel to cities, and
> before I show you anything I want to hear how you actually do it today. There are no
> right answers, and you can't hurt my feelings — the most useful thing you can do is
> tell me when something's confusing or pointless. OK if I record this?

Confirm: who they travelled with on their last city trip, and roughly when.

---

## 1 · How they plan today — 10 min

Tests **H1: planning is overwhelming and nobody ranks the options for you.**

**Lead:** "Tell me about the last trip you planned to a city you didn't know. Start from
the moment you decided to go."

**Probes** (use in any order, only as needed):
- "What did you do first?" → "And then?"
- "Where did the list of things to see come from?"
- "How did you decide what made the cut, and what didn't?"
- "Was there anything you wanted to do and dropped? Why?"
- "Who else weighed in? Who made the final call?"  ← listen for the Planner carrying it
- "How long did the planning take, all in?"
- "Was there a moment it felt like too much?" ← **only if they've already hinted at it**
- "What did you have in hand when you actually got there — a list, a spreadsheet, bookings?"

**Listen for (U only):** overwhelm, "everything says the same forty things", not trusting
reviews or being sold to, one person doing all the work, fear of wasting a day, wanting
someone to just tell them what to skip.

**Don't:** mention AI, audio, tours, or Ondoway.

---

## 2 · On the ground — 8 min

Tests **H2: underwhelmed during** and **H3: the day produces conversation afterwards.**

**Lead:** "Think of the last famous place you visited on a trip. Walk me through it — from
arriving to leaving."

**Probes:**
- "What do you remember about it now?"
- "What did you do while you were there? Read anything, listen to anything, look anything up?"
- "What did you do right after?"
- "Did you learn anything there that stuck with you?"
- **Dinner question:** "That evening, what did you all talk about?"
- "What were the others doing while you were looking at things?" ← kids, partner
- **Only at the end, only if nothing surfaced:** "Have you ever been somewhere famous and
  felt a bit underwhelmed?" → code **P**.

**Listen for (U only):** "smaller than I thought", "took a photo and left", "read the plaque,
didn't get it", queue boredom, kids bored or dragging, talking at dinner about the food and
the feet rather than the place — or, the opposite and equally important, a rich story they
still remember and who told it to them.

---

## Transition — 30 sec

> Thanks — that's really useful. Now I'm going to send you a link to something we're
> building. I'd like you to open it and share your screen. It's early, so some of it is
> pretend. Just go through it as if it were real, and **think out loud** — say what you
> expect, what you'd tap, what confuses you. I'm going to stay quiet and just ask the
> odd question.

Send the link. Open your interviewer view. From here on **do not click or narrate.**

---

## 3 · The walkthrough — 30 min

Questions per step. The traveller drives; ask only at the marked moments. The tap log
records what they touched; you record what they *said*.

**Step 1 · Lenses (2 min)**
- After they finish picking: "What do you think those choices will change?"
- "Were any of these obviously *not* you?"

**Step 2 · Planning the day (6 min)** — tests **H4: Skip advice is trusted**
- On the Day 2 plan, wait. Let them read.
- "What's your reaction to this?"
- "Where do you think these stops came from?" ← listens for "ads" / "someone paid"
- On the Skip advice card: "How do you feel about it telling you to skip that?"
- "Would you have found these in-between stops yourself?"
- **Comfort question:** "Would you be comfortable letting something plan your day like
  this? What would you need to see to trust it?" — probe any "no" to the reason.

**Step 3 · The walk (7 min)** — tests **H5: different stories per person**, **H6: Ask**
- Say nothing while the story plays. Watch whether they listen or skip.
- After the Federal Hall pair: "What do you make of that — the two of them standing in
  the same spot?" → "Would that be good or bad for your group? Why?"
- Do **not** mention the Ask button, the camera button or the lever buttons (Ask is always
  visible from 2026-09-18, so H6 measures use, not discovery). If never touched by the end of
  step 4: "Was there anything you wanted to know that it didn't tell you?" → code **P**.

**Step 4 · The day changes (3 min)**
- After the re-plan: "What just happened?" (tests whether it explained itself)
- "Is that what you'd want it to do? What would you have done without it?"

**Step 5 · In line — Deep dive + Echoes (5 min)** — tests **H7**, **H8**
- After a minute of the Deep dive: "Would you play this out loud, standing in the Statue
  of Liberty ferry line?" → probe the reason either way.
- After Echoes: "Would you leave one?" · "If these were only on a map, not in the camera,
  would you still bother?" · "Would you want to write anything you like here? Would you
  want your kids seeing what strangers wrote?"

**Step 6 · The kid in line — I Spy (2 min)** — tests **H9: the kid is the veto**
- "On your last trip, what did the kids do while you were looking at things?" (skip if
  already answered in part 2)
- "Would this change that?"

**Step 7 · The day and trip recap (5 min)** — tests **H3** (prompted), **H10**
- The photo-permission prompt: note what they chose and what they said before choosing.
- "What would you do with this?"
- "Is this something you'd look at at dinner, or later, or never?"
- Before asking about sharing, press **I just asked** in the interviewer view: a Share tap
  after it is coded P, before it U.
- "Would you share it? Where?"

---

## Close — 7 min, off the prototype

Share your own screen: the ranking board (seven cards, one per step, plus any cut step).

1. **Rank:** "If it could only do one of these, which one? Then the next?" — let them
   drag all seven.
2. **Alternative:** "For your top one — what would you use for that instead today?"
3. **Open price:** "For a trip like the one you told me about, what would this be worth to
   you?" — no numbers from you. Wait.
4. **Tiers:** show the two tiers (Solo/Pair $12–19 · Household $29–49, one city, one trip).
   "Which would you pick?"
5. **Coverage:** "Who in your group would it need to cover for that to feel fair?"
6. **Install:** "Would the others in your group actually install it?"
7. "What's the one thing you'd change?"
8. "Who else should I talk to?"

---

## After each session — 10 min, same day

Fill one row per session in the tally sheet (interviewer view exports it):

| Code | Hypothesis | Supports if… | Falsifier (from the deck, written in advance) |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Planning overwhelm | overwhelm / ranking pain raised **U** in part 1 | no planning pain unprompted |
| H2 | Underwhelmed during | disappointment raised **U** in part 2 | only a **P** yes |
| H3 | Dinner conversation | the day's places come up **U** at dinner; recap valued | talk is about food and feet; recap "never" |
| H4 | Skip advice trusted | positive or curious reaction; mentioned again at the close | nobody repeats a negative recommendation |
| H5 | Different stories per person | "we'd compare" / wants it for their group | "we'd each be on our phones" / lenses feel arbitrary |
| H6 | Sourced Q&A | "Ask" tapped **U** | never tapped, and no questions when prompted |
| H7 | Deep dive out loud | would play it aloud | would not; wants earbuds only |
| H8 | Echoes | would leave one; camera matters | wouldn't bother; map-only is fine |
| H9 | Kid is the veto | kids' boredom raised **U**; I Spy would change it | "kids wouldn't care" |
| H10 | Recap + photos | grants permission; would share | refuses permission; "never" |
| H11 | Willingness to pay | open price ≥ Solo/Pair; household tier feels fair | open price near zero; won't cover the party |

Also note: **every screen where you had to explain something** (prototype defects), and
the step they ranked first and last.

**Verdict rule:** after 6–8 sessions, a hypothesis is *supported* only if at least half of
the sessions support it and fewer than a quarter hit its falsifier. Anything else is
*unproven* — say so in the write-up, don't round up.
