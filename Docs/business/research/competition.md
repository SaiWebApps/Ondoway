# Competition: who a city visitor uses today, and where Ondoway wins

Research date: 2026-10-09. Sources are linked inline. "UNVERIFIED" means only a secondary or
single source supports it.

## The one-line answer

Everyone does one piece. Assistants and trip planners plan, but only answer when you ask.
Audio apps narrate, but play a fixed tour. Marketplaces sell tours, and are paid to. Nobody we
found ships all of it together: a day planned around you and your group, a guide that speaks
when you arrive, a day that re-plans itself when it changes, in the traveller's own language, and no booking
to sell you. Language is a gap for them: Siri AI launched English only, Ask Maps is US and
India only, Mindtrip covers 8 languages.

## The five groups

| Group | Who | Plans your day | Speaks when you arrive | Re-plans when the day changes | Plans for a group with different wishes | Paid to sell you something |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI assistants | ChatGPT, Gemini/Google Maps, Copilot, Perplexity, Meta AI glasses, Siri, Claude, Alexa+ | Yes, by chat | No, answers only when asked | Only if you type it out | No | Yes (ads, Expedia/Booking apps, hotel booking) |
| AI trip planners | Mindtrip, Layla (Expedia), Wanderlog, Trip.com TripGenie, Expedia, Booking, Airbnb, Tripadvisor | Yes | No (Mindtrip reads place notes aloud on request) | Partial (alerts, suggestions) | Partial (shared chats, voting) | Yes, almost all |

| Tour marketplaces | Viator, GetYourGuide, Headout, Klook, Airbnb Experiences, city passes | No | No (human guide, on their schedule) | No | No | Yes, 15–30% commission |
| Do it yourself | Blogs, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, friends | You do it | No | You do it | You do it | Creators and affiliate links |
| **Ondoway** | | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **No** |

## 1. AI assistants

**ChatGPT** is the one to beat in people's heads. 900M weekly users and 50M paying
([TechCrunch, 2026-02-27](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users)).
- Expedia and Booking.com apps inside ChatGPT since 2025-10-06
  ([Skift](https://skift.com/2025/10/06/expedia-booking-chatgpt-apps-openai/)); Tripadvisor, IHG,
  Iberia since. Booking sends you out to their site.
- Ads on Free and Go tiers, shown under answers
  ([OpenAI, 2026-01-16](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/)).
  Our own logged-out run showed an Expedia hotel ad and a tour-company ad under travel answers.
- Voice (GPT-Live, 2026-07-08) only answers when asked; you have to tell it you arrived. ChatGPT Maps reported in Europe in
  August 2026 (UNVERIFIED for the US).
- In our run: 5 messages and about 4,600 words to reach a family plan; on the street it
  had to be told we had arrived and guessed where we were ("almost certainly at Les Invalides"); re-planning needed us to type
  out the problem, and it did not know the day of the week.

**Google** is the biggest threat.
- "Gemini can be your personal walking tour guide" in Maps (2026-01-29), but every example is a
  question you ask ([blog.google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/gemini-navigation-biking-walking/)).
- Ask Maps builds itineraries, US and India (2026-03-12)
  ([blog.google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-immersive-navigation/)).
- Hotel booking inside AI Mode with 10 partners (2026-08-27)
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/googles-ai-mode-can-now-track-flight-prices-help-book-hotels-and-more/)).
- Android XR audio glasses due in the US this fall; a developer demo has a voice describing
  landmarks in front of you, "for demonstration purposes only"
  ([Android Developers Blog, 2026-06-17](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/android-xr-geospatial-api-gemini.html)).

**Meta**: Ray-Ban Meta glasses answer "look and tell me" questions; Live AI ran about 30
minutes a charge at launch. Connect 2026 (2026-09-23) added landmark-based walking directions
and an Expedia connector "coming soon". No tour feature
([meta.com](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/)).

**Others**: Copilot (booking partners Booking, Expedia, Viator and more), Perplexity (hotel
booking via Selfbook), Siri AI beta (English only, waitlist, 2026-09-14), Claude (Booking,
Tripadvisor, Expedia connectors), Alexa+ (Expedia hotel booking). None guides you on the street.

## 2. AI trip planners

- **Mindtrip** — the closest planner. Plans days, group chat that will "balance everyone's
  vibes", voice descriptions of places on request, arrival notifications, 8 languages. Free;
  earns from bookings (Expedia, Booking, Priceline, Agoda). $22.5M raised.
  4.7★ from 828 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindtrip-ai-travel-companion/id6503107567)).
  It does not narrate as you walk or re-plan your day by itself.
- **Layla** — bought by Expedia, 2026-07-31. 5M+ users, $9.99/month
  ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/07/31/expedia-acquired-ai-trip-planner-layla-exclusive)).
- **Wanderlog** — group itineraries, route optimisation, 4.9★ from 36K ratings, $39.99/year.
  No in-trip guide.
- **Trip.com TripGenie, Expedia, Booking.com, Kayak, Airbnb, Tripadvisor** — all add AI
  planning to sell bookings. Airbnb's CEO called AI trip planning "not ready for prime time" in
  2025; its 2026 releases add a shared itinerary map, not a guide.
- **Small ones**: Airial ($3M seed), Holiwise, Stippl, Polarsteps (20M travellers, a tracker
  not a guide).

More audio and guide apps (none plans a day or re-plans):
- **Rick Steves Audio Europe** — free, English only, Europe, no NYC. 4.2★/509.
- **Bloomberg Connects** — free museum guides in 61 languages; Met, MoMA, Guggenheim, Whitney in
  NYC (UNVERIFIED list). This is why free guides win inside a museum.
- **Smartify** — museum audio, 700+ partner organisations, £1.5M seed (Jan 2025).
- **GuideAlong, Shaka Guide, Action Tour Guide** — GPS driving tours, mostly parks; Action has
  an NYC tour.
- **izi.TRAVEL** — 25,000 tours, 50+ languages, sells tickets.
- **GuideGeek** (Matador Network) — free AI chat assistant inside WhatsApp, Instagram and
  Messenger, built on OpenAI; 1.5M+ users (company figure); white-label versions for
  destinations, NYC among them. Chat only: no GPS narration, no live re-plan
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GuideGeek)).
- **Apple Maps Guides** — curated place lists only; no audio or tour feature found in iOS 26/27
  announcements.

Notes:
- GPS narration alone is not new. The combination is.
- Nobody plans for a group whose members want different things, and nobody tells you what to
  skip and why.
- VoiceMap now markets itself as "curiously human" against AI tours (Stephen Fry, Dan Snow,
  May 2026).
- Dex is free and owned by a marketplace: it exists to sell Headout tickets.

## Who goes on the competition slide

Five names a room recognises, one per group, plus the closest specialist:
1. **ChatGPT** — what people use today.
2. **Google Maps + Gemini** — the giant moving toward a walking guide.
3. **Mindtrip** — the best AI planner.
4. **Headout Dex** — free audio guide, live in NYC, owned by a ticket seller.
5. **Viator** — the default way to buy a tour.

## The 2×2

- **Across:** "fixed tour" → "plans around you and your group".
- **Up:** "before the trip" → "with you on the street".

| | Fixed tour | Plans around you |
|---|---|---|
| **With you on the street** | VoiceMap, Routes, Dex, Viator guides | **Ondoway** |
| **Before the trip** | Blogs, Viator listings, city passes | ChatGPT, Gemini, Mindtrip, Layla |

A third mark on each dot can show "paid to sell you something": everyone except Ondoway and
VoiceMap.
