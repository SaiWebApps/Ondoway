# Ondoway Traveller

What a traveller plans, walks and keeps: trips, tours and the stories they hear on the
way. This glossary is the language of the app side; it consumes the beats the corpus
defines.

## Planning

**Planner**:
The party member who organises the trip and buys the pass: typically 3–5 days in an
unfamiliar city with 2–5 people, carrying everyone else's day. Ondoway's customer.
_Avoid_: user, lead traveller, parent, persona

**Lens**:
An interest angle a traveller chooses (history, architecture, food) that decides which
beats they hear. Beats are tagged with lenses; travellers select them.
_Avoid_: category, theme, tag, interest

**Trip**:
A traveller's visit to one city across one or more days, grouping every tour built for
it.
_Avoid_: itinerary (the word travellers use for the plan a trip shows them), vacation

**Tour**:
One walk inside one free time window, with an ordered set of stops and the stories
heard on the way.
_Avoid_: route (the geometry only), walk, itinerary

**Must-see**:
A site the traveller says they already intend to visit. A trip may hold several;
Ondoway plans around them rather than choosing them.
_Avoid_: anchor, bucket list, favourite

**Anchor**:
The directional target one tour walks toward, set by the engine. Not a promise to end
there, and never a synonym for must-see.
_Avoid_: destination, endpoint, must-see

**Stop reason**:
The one-line, traveller-specific "why this is here for you" attached to every stop
Ondoway adds. The visible answer to "is this app just selling me something."
_Avoid_: description, blurb, tagline

**Skip advice**:
Ondoway recommending against visiting a site, or against visiting it as planned, with
the reason and an alternative. Advice only: the traveller keeps the final word.
_Avoid_: removal, veto, filter

## On the ground

**Stop story**:
A short story that fires by proximity as the traveller walks up to a stop.
_Avoid_: clip, track, beat (the corpus unit it is built from)

**I Spy**:
A child's find-it prompt at a stop, on the child's own view of the same tour, so the
youngest member of the party has something to do while the adults listen.
_Avoid_: kids mode, game, scavenger hunt

**Deep dive**:
A long-form story about a must-see, heard while the party waits before going in (in a
queue, on a bench), sized to the length of the wait, so that what they see inside
means something. One story for the whole party, heard together; the one place a party
converges instead of hearing its own lenses.
_Avoid_: audiobook mode, queue story, queue chapter, long listen

**Re-plan**:
A tour changing its remaining stops mid-walk because time ran short or the traveller
asked, keeping must-sees and saying what it dropped and why.
_Avoid_: reroute (that is navigation only), adjustment

**Sourced answer**:
Ondoway's reply to a traveller's question about where they stand, drawn only from the
beats at that place. When those beats do not cover it, it says so rather than guess.
_Avoid_: chat, AI answer, search

## Afterwards

**Tour close**:
The calm end-of-walk summary shown when one tour finishes: route, time, stops, a
rating. Deliberately quiet.
_Avoid_: celebration, wrap-up

**Day recap**:
The evening screen that tells the party's day back to it: route, matched photos, the
questions asked, and each person's different story from the same place side by side,
so the party has something to trade at dinner.
_Avoid_: journal, summary, day log

**Trip recap**:
The once-per-trip, shareable retrospective built after the trip from the traveller's
stories, echoes, lenses and (with permission) their own photos, with a keepsake
export.
_Avoid_: wrapped, completion summary (that is the tour close), memory reel

## Shared

**Party**:
The people travelling one trip together, each on their own phone with their own
lenses, linked by invitation. They walk the same tours and may hear different stories.
_Avoid_: group, family, account, household (a billing idea, not a travel one)

**Trip pass**:
What a traveller buys: one city, one trip, paid once, never renewed. Sold as solo/pair
or household; the tour is how Ondoway is found, never what is in the cart.
_Avoid_: subscription, city unlock, tour purchase, ARR

**Lens profile**:
A set of lenses kept under someone else's account for a person without their own
phone (a young child). The fallback when a party member cannot be linked.
_Avoid_: profile (alone), sub-account, user

**Echo**:
A short note a traveller leaves at an exact spot, assembled only from fixed templates
and word lists, discovered by later travellers through the phone's camera view, who
respond with fixed reactions. Traveller-made and unverified, so never voiced by the
narrator and never part of a story.
_Avoid_: pin (a map marker), message, graffiti, review, trace
